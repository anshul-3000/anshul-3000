## 🔬 Research Profile

I am a Computer Science researcher and **M.Tech student** interested in developing intelligent systems using Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Generative AI, Natural Language Processing, and Computer Vision.

My primary focus is on **understanding and implementing modern AI architectures** rather than simply using existing models. I am particularly interested in representation learning, generative modeling, latent-space learning, diffusion models, multimodal learning, and AI for biological applications.

Currently, I am exploring research problems involving:

- 🧠 Deep Learning & Representation Learning
- 🤖 Generative AI & Diffusion Models
- 🧬 AI for Protein & Antimicrobial Peptide Research
- 🗣️ Natural Language Processing
- 👁️ Computer Vision
- 📊 Machine Learning & Data Analysis
- ⚡ Efficient & Edge AI
- 🔬 Experimental AI Research & Reproducibility

> **Research Philosophy:** Understand the mathematics → implement the method → reproduce the results → identify limitations → propose improvements.

## 🧬 Selected Research Projects

### 🔬 AMP Latent Diffusion

Research project exploring **latent diffusion for antimicrobial peptide generation**.

**Research Components:**

- Transformer-based sequence representation
- Variational Autoencoder
- Latent-space extraction
- Diffusion-based generation
- Peptide sequence modeling
- Biological property evaluation

*Focus: Investigating whether learning a structured latent representation can improve the quality and controllability of generated peptide sequences.*

---

### 🚗 SafeDrive AI

A real-time computer vision system for **driver drowsiness detection**.

**Research Components:**

- OpenCV
- Haar Cascade
- Eye Aspect Ratio
- Dynamic threshold calibration
- Real-time video processing

📄 **Research Paper:** Published at NICEDT 2025

---

### 😊 MoodMap

Real-time **facial emotion recognition** system using Convolutional Neural Networks.

**Research Components:**

- CNN-based classification
- Facial image preprocessing
- OpenCV
- TensorFlow/Keras
- Real-time inference

---
